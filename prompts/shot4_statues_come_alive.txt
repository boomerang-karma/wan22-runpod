Indoors in a bright hall with a white marble floor, two young girls stand perfectly still in front of a bronze sculpture of three seated children, copying the statues: the girl in the middle, in a blue top and dark tulle skirt, keeps both hands pressed over her eyes the whole time, and the girl on the right, in a cream party dress with a sparkly headband, keeps both hands pressed over her mouth the whole time. The girls never lower their hands; they only giggle silently behind them, their shoulders shaking a little and the right girl's eyes crinkling with laughter. Behind them, the three bronze statues magically come alive: slowly and smoothly, the statue covering its ears lowers its hands to its lap, the statue covering its eyes lowers its hands and blinks, and the statue covering its mouth lowers its hands, then all three statues glance at the girls and gently lift their hands back to cover their ears, eyes and mouth again, returning to their original pose, still made of shiny bronze. Camera locked off, natural indoor lighting, warm, magical, playful family moment. Sound: soft echo of a large indoor hall, a faint metallic creak as the bronze statues move, muffled giggles behind hands.
